Simple idea

The bot gives each move a score.
Then it picks the move with the highest score.
Basic equation
Move Score=(captures)+(checks)+(king attack)−(risk)
Move Score=(captures)+(checks)+(king attack)−(risk)

Each bot profile changes what matters most.
Offensive bot

This bot likes taking pieces.
Score=5(captures)+2(checks)−1(risk)
Score=5(captures)+2(checks)−1(risk)

Meaning:

    capturing pieces is very important,

    checks matter a little,

    risk matters a little too.

So it chooses moves that win pieces, even if the move is a bit unsafe.
Safe bot

This bot likes staying protected.
Score=1(captures)−4(risk)−3(king danger)
Score=1(captures)−4(risk)−3(king danger)

Meaning:

    captures are okay,

    but danger is very bad,

    king safety matters a lot.

So it prefers moves that keep its pieces and king safe.
Strategic bot

This bot likes attacking the enemy king.
Score=2(captures)+4(king attack)+3(checks)−1(risk)
Score=2(captures)+4(king attack)+3(checks)−1(risk)

Meaning:

    captures are useful,

    attacking the king is very important,

    checks are also very important.

So it chooses moves that build pressure near the enemy king.
What the bot does

For every legal move, it calculates a score like this:
best move=move with the biggest score
best move=move with the biggest score

So all three bots use the same idea, but they care about different things.

    Offensive = “I want pieces.”

    Safe = “I want protection.”

    Strategic = “I want to attack the king.”
